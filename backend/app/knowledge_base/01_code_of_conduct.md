# Northstar Innovation — Code of Conduct

## Purpose and scope
This Code of Conduct applies to all Northstar Innovation employees, contractors,
and interns. It sets the baseline expectations for professional behaviour and
must be acknowledged in writing during onboarding and re-acknowledged every 12
months.

## Professional behaviour
Employees are expected to treat colleagues, customers, and partners with respect.
Harassment, discrimination, and retaliation are strictly prohibited. Violations
may result in disciplinary action up to and including termination.

## Conflicts of interest
Employees must disclose any outside activity, investment, or relationship that
could reasonably conflict with their duties at Northstar. Disclosures are
submitted to the People team and reviewed within ten business days. Employees may
not supervise a family member or a person with whom they have a close personal
relationship.

## Outside employment
Employees may hold outside employment only if it does not compete with Northstar,
does not use Northstar resources, and does not interfere with their job duties.
Outside employment involving a competitor, vendor, or customer requires written
approval from the People team.

## Gifts and entertainment
Employees may not accept gifts valued at more than 100 dollars from any vendor or
partner. Gifts above this value must be declined or reported to a manager. Cash
and cash equivalents such as gift cards may never be accepted, regardless of
value.

## Use of company resources
Company equipment, accounts, and funds are provided for business purposes.
Limited personal use of company devices is permitted so long as it is lawful,
minimal, and does not interfere with work.

## Confidentiality
Employees must protect confidential company and customer information both during
and after their employment. Confidential information may only be shared with
people who have a legitimate business need to know.

## Reporting concerns
Suspected violations of this Code should be reported to a manager or to the
People team. Reports may be made anonymously through the internal ethics line.
Northstar prohibits retaliation against anyone who reports a concern in good
faith. Reported concerns are acknowledged within 2 business days and investigated
promptly.
