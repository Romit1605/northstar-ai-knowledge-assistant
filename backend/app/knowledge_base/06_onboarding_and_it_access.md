# Northstar Innovation — Onboarding and IT Access

## First day
New employees complete identity verification and sign the Code of Conduct on
their first day. A People team representative guides new hires through
orientation and introduces them to their team.

## IT account provisioning
IT accounts and email are provisioned within 2 business days of a signed offer.
Standard software and hardware are issued during the first week. New hires
receive a laptop, a company email account, and access to core collaboration
tools by default.

## Requesting additional access
Requests for access to additional systems are submitted through the IT portal
and require manager approval. Most access requests are fulfilled within 3
business days. Access to systems containing confidential data may require
additional Security team approval.

## Equipment
Standard equipment includes a laptop and one external monitor. Requests for
additional or specialized equipment require manager approval. Lost or damaged
equipment must be reported to IT within 2 business days.

## Training requirements
All new employees must complete security awareness training within their first
14 days. Failure to complete required training may result in temporary
suspension of system access. Role-specific training is assigned by the
employee's manager.

## Probation period
New employees serve a probation period of 90 days, during which performance and
fit are reviewed. A check-in with the manager is scheduled at 30, 60, and 90
days.

## Offboarding
When an employee leaves, their manager notifies IT and the People team. All
system access is revoked within 24 hours of the employee's last day, and company
equipment must be returned within 5 business days. Final pay is issued according
to local employment regulations.
