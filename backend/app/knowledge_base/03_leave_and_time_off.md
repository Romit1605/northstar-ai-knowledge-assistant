# Northstar Innovation — Leave and Time Off

## Paid time off
Full-time employees accrue 20 days of paid time off (PTO) per calendar year.
PTO accrues monthly and is available to use once accrued. Part-time employees
accrue PTO on a pro-rated basis according to their scheduled hours.

## Carryover
Employees may carry over a maximum of 5 unused PTO days into the following
calendar year. Days beyond this limit are forfeited on 31 December. Carried-over
days must be used by 31 March or they expire.

## Requesting time off
PTO requests must be submitted through the HR portal at least 10 business days in
advance for planned absences. Managers approve or decline requests within 3
business days. During peak business periods, managers may limit the number of
team members on leave at the same time.

## Sick leave
Employees receive 8 paid sick days per year, separate from PTO. A doctor's note
is required for any sick absence longer than 3 consecutive days. Unused sick days
do not carry over to the next year.

## Bereavement leave
Employees are entitled to up to 5 days of paid bereavement leave for the death of
an immediate family member, and up to 2 days for an extended family member.

## Parental leave
Eligible employees are entitled to 16 weeks of paid parental leave following one
year of continuous employment. Requests should be submitted at least 30 days
before the expected leave start date. Parental leave may be taken within 12
months of the birth or adoption of a child.

## Public holidays
Northstar observes 11 public holidays per year. Employees required to work on a
public holiday receive an alternate day off in lieu, to be taken within 60 days.

## Unpaid leave
Employees may request unpaid personal leave for exceptional circumstances.
Unpaid leave of more than 5 consecutive days requires manager and People team
approval.
