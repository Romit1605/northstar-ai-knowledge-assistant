# Northstar Innovation — Remote Work Policy

## Eligibility
Full-time employees who have completed their first 30 days are eligible for
hybrid remote work, subject to manager approval. Roles that require on-site
presence, such as facilities and hardware lab roles, may not be eligible for
remote work.

## In-office expectations
Hybrid employees are expected to work from a Northstar office at least two days
per week. Teams may set a shared in-office day for collaboration. Fully remote
arrangements are granted only by exception and require director approval.

## Home office equipment
Northstar provides a one-time home office equipment stipend of 500 dollars for
newly approved remote employees. The stipend is requested through the IT portal
and reimbursed with the next payroll cycle. Employees may request an ergonomic
assessment at no cost.

## Working hours and availability
Remote employees are expected to be available during core hours of 10:00 to
15:00 local time. Any change to an employee's primary work location must be
approved by their manager at least five business days in advance. Employees must
keep their calendar and status up to date during working hours.

## Working from a different location
Employees who wish to work temporarily from a location outside their usual
province or country must obtain approval at least 10 business days in advance,
as this may have tax and security implications. Temporary out-of-country work is
limited to a maximum of 20 business days per calendar year.

## Security while remote
Remote employees must connect through the company VPN when accessing internal
systems and must not use public shared computers for company work. All devices
must have full-disk encryption enabled. Employees must not allow family members
or others to use company devices.

## Meetings and collaboration
Remote employees are expected to join video meetings with their camera on for
team ceremonies unless otherwise agreed. Important decisions made in meetings
should be documented in the team's shared workspace so on-site and remote
employees have equal access to information.
