# Northstar Innovation — Expense and Procurement Policy

## Expense reimbursement
Employees must submit expense reports within 30 days of incurring a business
expense. Reports submitted after 30 days may not be reimbursed. Reimbursements
are paid with the next scheduled payroll run after approval.

## Approval thresholds
Business expenses up to 250 dollars may be approved by the employee's direct
manager. Expenses above 250 dollars require additional approval from a
department lead. Expenses above 5,000 dollars require finance team approval.

## Travel
Economy airfare and standard hotel rates should be used for business travel.
Employees are reimbursed for meals up to a daily limit of 75 dollars while
travelling. Travel that exceeds 1,500 dollars in total must be approved in
advance.

## Ground transportation
Employees should use standard rideshare, taxi, or public transit for local
business travel. Rental cars require manager approval and should be economy or
mid-size class.

## Purchasing software and tools
New software purchases must be reviewed by the IT team before purchase to check
for security and licensing compliance. Requests are reviewed within 5 business
days. Recurring subscriptions must be registered with IT so licenses can be
tracked and renewed.

## Vendor selection
Purchases above 10,000 dollars generally require at least two competing quotes.
Preferred vendors should be used where available. New vendors must complete a
security and privacy review before onboarding.

## Corporate cards
Corporate cards are issued to employees who travel frequently. Card holders must
reconcile their transactions monthly and attach receipts. Personal use of a
corporate card is prohibited.

## Non-reimbursable expenses
Personal entertainment, fines, and unapproved upgrades are not reimbursable.
Receipts are required for any expense of 25 dollars or more.
