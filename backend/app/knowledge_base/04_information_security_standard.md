# Northstar Innovation — Information Security Standard

## Purpose
This standard defines the minimum security controls that all employees must
follow to protect Northstar systems and data. It applies to all company-owned
and approved personal devices used for work.

## Passwords and authentication
Employees must use unique passwords of at least 12 characters for all company
accounts. Passwords must be rotated every 90 days. Multi-factor authentication
is required for all systems that support it. Passwords must never be shared or
reused across personal and company accounts.

## Data classification
Company data is classified as Public, Internal, or Confidential. Confidential
data must never be sent to personal email accounts or stored on unapproved
cloud services. Internal data may be shared within Northstar but not published
externally without approval.

## Data handling and storage
Confidential data at rest must be stored only on approved, encrypted systems.
Employees must not copy confidential data to personal USB drives. Printed
documents containing confidential data must be stored securely and shredded when
no longer needed.

## Security incident reporting
Suspected security incidents must be reported to the Security team within one
hour of discovery. Employees must not attempt to investigate or remediate an
incident on their own before reporting it. Examples of security incidents include
lost or stolen devices, suspected phishing, and unauthorized access to accounts.

## Phishing and email safety
Employees must not click links or open attachments from untrusted or unexpected
emails. Suspected phishing emails must be reported using the "Report Phishing"
button and must not be forwarded to colleagues.

## Access control
Access to systems is granted on a least-privilege basis. Access reviews are
performed quarterly, and access is revoked within 24 hours of an employee
leaving the company. Shared accounts are prohibited unless formally approved by
the Security team.

## Device security
All laptops must have full-disk encryption, an approved antivirus agent, and
automatic screen lock after 10 minutes of inactivity. Employees must apply
security updates within 7 days of release.

## Acceptable use
Company devices may not be used to store or share unauthorized copyrighted
material. Employees must lock their screens when away from their desks and must
not disable required security software.
